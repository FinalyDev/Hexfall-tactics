How to play
• Tap shop cards to buy units. Drag them from the bench onto the bottom half of the board.
• All 8 players share a limited pool of each unit (18/15/13/10/8 copies by cost). Units in shops are reserved until rerolled, so contested units run out.
• 3 copies of a unit combine into a ★★ unit; 3 ★★ make a ★★★. Shop cards show ⚔ how many you have in play, 🪑 on your bench, dots for copies, and glow gold with ⬆★★ when buying ranks it up.
• Every unit has an origin and a class. Field 2, 4 or 6 different units of a trait to unlock its special power — burns, chain lightning, revives, stuns, taunts and more. Tap a trait to read it. Each set has its own traits.
• Assassins (sets 2–4) leap onto the enemy back line when the fight starts.
• Your level = how many units can fight. Every 10 gold you hold earns +1 interest (max 5).
• Monster rounds drop units, gold and item parts (shown in your 🎒 bag above the shop). Drag one part onto another in your bag (or tap it) to craft a full item, then drag it onto a unit.
• Every full item has a special effect (tap it to read). From stage 3, monster rounds drop an ⚒️ Artifact Anvil — tap it to forge a one-of-a-kind artifact.
• Streaks: 2+ wins or losses in a row (🔥/❄️) pay up to +3 bonus gold each round.
• At round 4 of every stage (from stage 2) there's a 🎠 carousel: lowest health picks first from units carrying items.
• You can buy, reroll and level up during fights — new units go to your bench and combine when the fight ends.
• At 2-1, 3-2 and 4-2 you pick an augment card — a Silver, Gold or Prismatic power-up for the rest of the game.
• Drag a unit onto the shop to sell it. Tap anything to see its stats.
• Lose a fight and you lose HP. Be the last one standing!
