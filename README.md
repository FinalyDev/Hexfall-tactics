# Hexfall Tactics

An offline, single-player auto-battler that runs in your browser. Draft units from a shop, combine 3 copies to rank them up, stack traits, build items from monster drops, and outlast 7 AI rivals.

- Works offline once loaded (installable as an app: "Add to Home Screen").
- Progress auto-saves at the start of each planning phase.
- Desktop shortcuts: **D** reroll, **F** buy XP, **Space** fight / change speed.

Four sets to choose from: **Elements**, **Neon Circuit**, **Moonlit Kingdom** and **Sweet Treats** — each with 41 units (10 one-costs), its own traits, colours and monsters. All characters are original anime-style chibi heroes (idle, blink, attack and casting poses) and original monsters, drawn in code.

Also: craft items right in your bag, and pick Silver / Gold / Prismatic **augments** at 2-1, 3-2 and 4-2.


## Items
Every completed item has a special effect (attack speed per hit or per second, splash, chain lightning, burn, thorns, shields, lifesteal and more). From stage 3, monster rounds drop an **Artifact Anvil** to forge one of 10 one-of-a-kind artifacts (Phoenix Feather, Echo Crystal, Stormcaller Horn…). Fights that drag past 25s go into **overtime**: damage ramps up and healing is halved.
